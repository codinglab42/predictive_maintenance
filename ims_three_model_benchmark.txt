ALARM AND MODEL-SELECTION POLICY
--------------------------------
For each model, probabilities are smoothed independently per bearing using a
causal rolling mean of 5 acquisitions (or fewer at the beginning). An alarm
is confirmed after 3 consecutive smoothed values strictly above that model's
threshold.

Threshold candidates are evaluated on 2nd_test. Selection maximizes F1 of the
confirmed alarms, then minimizes false-alarm episodes on healthy Bearings 2-4,
then prefers the higher threshold. The dashboard default model maximizes F1,
then minimizes healthy-bearing false alarms, then prefers lower inference
time.

INTERPRETATION
--------------
Thresholds and reported metrics were selected/evaluated using labels from
2nd_test. These are exploratory retrospective benchmark results, not an
independent test result, industrial validation, or calibrated failure
probabilities. The positive tail is a proxy label, not an observed failure
time. Alarm outputs require human review.

Timing covers model fitting and predict_proba only. It excludes feature
extraction, scaling, metric calculation, and artifact writes. Timings are
wall-clock measurements and can vary by machine.
